# Expense Reimbursement Guide

How to get expenses reimbursed quickly, and what the finance team checks.

## Monthly cycle

Expense reports are due by the 15th of the following month. Reports approved by the 20th
are paid in that month's final payroll run; later approvals roll to the next cycle. Your
manager approves the report first, then finance reviews receipts.

## Meals and daily limits

The meal cap is 40 dollars per day for domestic travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Team
meals during offsites are booked centrally and never appear on personal expense reports.
When a client joins a meal, note the client names on the receipt for the higher client-
entertainment rules.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment above 200 dollars needs pre-approval from a director before the
event. Attach the attendee list and the business purpose. Entertainment for prospective
clients follows the same rule and must also be logged for the compliance gift register
when combined gift value exceeds 50 dollars per person per year.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: minibar charges, traffic and parking fines, airline
club memberships, seat upgrades, and personal entertainment during travel. Lost receipt
affidavits are allowed twice per year for expenses under 25 dollars.

## Foreign currency and mileage

Foreign-currency expenses are converted at the card network rate automatically; do not
type your own conversion rate. Personal-car trips are claimed as mileage using the current
mileage rate rather than fuel receipts. The mileage claim and the fuel-reimbursement
methods cannot be mixed for the same trip.

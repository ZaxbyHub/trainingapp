# IT Helpdesk Guide

The IT helpdesk is the single entry point for hardware, software, and account support.

## Accounts and passwords

Password resets are self-service through the helpdesk portal; click "Reset password" and
follow the emailed link. The link expires after 30 minutes. Accounts lock automatically
after 5 failed login attempts and unlock themselves after 15 minutes. Locked accounts that
do not self-unlock need a helpdesk ticket with the subject line "Account lockout".

## VPN and network access

Remote access requires the corporate VPN client with multi-factor authentication enabled.
The VPN profile name is CORP-SECURE. The office wifi has two networks: CORP-SECURE for
managed devices and GUEST-NET for visitors; guest access codes change weekly and are
available at the reception desk.

## Hardware and software

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years; you will receive an email 60 days before your refresh
date. Software that is not in the self-service catalog requires a ticket in the "Software
request" queue; approvals take up to 5 business days. Docking stations, monitors, and
keyboards can be picked up from the IT storeroom on floor 2 without a ticket.

## Printing and support hours

Printer support is available on weekdays from 08:00 to 18:00. Outside those hours, use the
secure-print release stations, which queue jobs until you badge in. For anything else,
the helpdesk portal lists the current queue status and the on-call engineer for urgent
outages.

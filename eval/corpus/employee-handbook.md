# Employee Handbook

Welcome to the company. This handbook summarizes the employment terms that apply to all
full-time staff. Contractors follow the terms in their statement of work instead.

## Working hours and remote work

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Outside those hours
teams arrange schedules with their managers. Employees may work remotely up to three days
per week; fully remote arrangements require a written agreement with the department head.

## Vacation and leave

New employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. After five years
of service the allowance increases by 5 additional days. Up to 5 unused vacation days roll
over into the next calendar year; the rest are paid out in January.

Sick leave is 8 days per year and does not accrue across years. Parental leave grants
16 weeks of paid time for birthing parents and 8 weeks for non-birthing parents. Jury duty
and bereavement leave are paid and do not reduce the vacation balance.

## Probation and reviews

The first 90 days of employment are a probation period with two check-ins. After probation,
performance reviews run twice a year, in March and September. Promotion cases are evaluated
in the September cycle.

## Equipment and workspace

Every employee receives a laptop, a monitor, and a stipend for a desk chair. Office
parking is assigned by seniority; there is no charge for bicycle storage. The fitness room
in the basement is open from 06:00 to 21:00.

## Conduct basics

Employees must complete workplace-conduct training within their first month. Violations of
the code of conduct are handled by the people team under the process in the compliance
documentation.

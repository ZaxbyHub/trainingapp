# Compliance Essentials

This document summarizes the compliance obligations that apply to every employee.

## Annual training and attestations

Annual compliance training must be completed by December 31 each year. The code of conduct
attestation is signed during onboarding and re-signed with each annual training. Managers
with direct reports also complete the manager addendum module on handling complaints.

## Gifts and entertainment

Gifts from vendors or customers above 50 dollars in value must be declined or, when
declining is impractical, registered in the gift register within 5 business days. Cash
gifts are never acceptable regardless of amount. Hospitality such as conference tickets or
meals must be business-related and pre-approved when the value exceeds 200 dollars.

## Records and retention

Business records are retained for 7 years unless a records-hold notice says otherwise.
When a hold is issued, all deletion of matching records stops immediately, including
automatic retention cleanup. Email is archived automatically; local archives are not
permitted.

## Reporting concerns

The whistleblower hotline is available around the clock and accepts anonymous reports.
Reports can also be made directly to the compliance officer. Retaliation against anyone
who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of the code of conduct and is
treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

## Anti-bribery

Bribes and facilitation payments are prohibited in every country where the company
operates, even where local practice tolerates them. Third parties acting for the company
are subject to the same anti-bribery standard through contractual clauses and diligence
screening.

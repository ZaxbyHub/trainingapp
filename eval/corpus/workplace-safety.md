# Workplace Safety Manual

This manual describes the safety program for all office locations.

## Emergency procedures

Emergency exit maps are posted next to every elevator and stairwell door. Evacuation
assembly points are in the parking lot's north corner. Do not use elevators during a fire
alarm. Floor wardens wear orange vests during drills and real evacuations.

## Fire and equipment safety

Fire extinguishers are inspected on a monthly schedule; the inspection tag shows the last
check date. Report a missing or discharged extinguisher to the facilities team the same
day. Space heaters are not permitted at desks; the only approved heating devices are the
ones facilities installed.

## First aid

First aid kits are mounted in every kitchen and in the gym. The defibrillator (AED) is next
to the reception desk on floor 1. Trained first responders are listed on the safety board;
at least two are on site during business hours.

## Incident reporting

Any workplace accident, however minor, must be reported within 24 hours through the safety
reporting form. Near-miss events are reported on the same form — near-miss reports are used
to fix hazards before someone is hurt. Injuries requiring medical attention also need a
call to the facilities emergency line.

## Ergonomics and drills

Ergonomic workstation assessments are available on request and are recommended once per
year. Fire evacuation drills run quarterly; participation is mandatory and recorded. The
safety committee reviews all reports monthly and posts corrective actions on the safety
board.

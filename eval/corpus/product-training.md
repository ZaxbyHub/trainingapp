# Atlas CRM Product Training Guide

This guide supports the Atlas CRM training curriculum. Atlas CRM is the customer
relationship platform used by the sales and success teams.

## Product modules

Atlas CRM ships two core modules. Sales Pulse manages pipeline stages, quotes, and deal
forecasting. Insight Board renders dashboards for win rate, cycle time, and churn risk
using the same underlying data. Both modules share the global search bar and the customer
timeline.

## Release cadence and environments

Releases go out on a quarterly cadence, with patch releases as needed between quarters.
Every customer works in a production tenant and can request a sandbox tenant for training;
sandboxes refresh from production snapshots every Sunday and are not for storing real
customer data.

## Certification program

The certification program has two levels. The Associate level covers navigation, record
management, and reporting basics. The Professional level adds workflow automation, quota
forecasting, and admin configuration. Each certification exam takes 60 minutes, and a
failed exam can be retaken after 7 days.

## Training resources

The training portal hosts self-paced courses for each module. Live instructor sessions run
twice a month and are recorded. New sales hires complete the Associate curriculum during
their onboarding weeks; the CRM team tracks completion and reports it to hiring managers.

## Support model

Product questions go to the CRM help channel first. Defects are filed in the product tracker
with reproduction steps and tenant id. Enhancement requests are collected quarterly by the
product team and prioritized in the public roadmap review.

# Business Travel Policy

This policy covers all work-related travel booked by employees. All travel must be arranged
through the corporate travel portal. Domestic flights should be booked at least 14 days
before departure; international trips should be booked 21 days ahead to secure the
negotiated fares.

## Air travel

For flights under six hours, economy class is the standard. Flights of six hours or longer
qualify for premium economy. Business class requires written pre-approval from a vice
president and is granted only for non-stop flights longer than ten hours.

## Ground transport and mileage

When a personal vehicle is used for business travel, mileage is reimbursed at 0.655 dollars
per mile. The mileage rate is reviewed every January against the national standard.
Rideshare services may be used without pre-approval for trips under 30 dollars; longer
rideshare trips require a trip purpose note on the expense report.

## Hotels and per diem

Hotel stays are capped at 180 dollars per night in domestic markets. There is no separate
per diem for domestic trips; meals fall under the standard expense meal cap. International
assignments include a per diem of 85 dollars per day, which covers meals and local
transport. Laundry service is reimbursable on trips longer than five nights.

## Receipts and reimbursement deadlines

Itemized receipts are required for any single expense of 25 dollars or more. Expense
reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date; later submissions require a
late-justification memo from the traveler's manager. Reimbursement is paid with the next
regular payroll cycle after approval.

## Cancellations and changes

Flight change fees caused by personal schedule changes are not reimbursed. Change fees
caused by project schedule changes are reimbursed with manager confirmation. Refundable
fares may be booked for trips expected to shift by more than two days.

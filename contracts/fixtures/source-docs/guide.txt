Packing guide

This plain-text guide walks through the three packtool verbs added for
issue 73. build-docs extracts, chunks, and embeds every supported document
in a source folder and writes a conformant pack.json plus a prebuilt
index.sqlite with FTS. verify re-hashes every document and confirms the
index stamps. diff reports added, removed, and changed documents plus the
chunk-count delta between two packs.
